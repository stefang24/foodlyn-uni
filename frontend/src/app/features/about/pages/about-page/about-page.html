Foodlyn is a modern restaurant operating system - QR ordering, kitchen displays, cashier flow and manager insights in one place. It's built, designed and shipped by Lynflare, a small product studio that loves hospitality software.

Lynflare

We're a lean software company focused on hospitality tooling. We believe restaurants deserve software that feels like a native app - fast, obvious, and reliable during a Saturday-night rush.

Foodlyn

Foodlyn is our flagship product. One platform for guests, waiters, cooks, cashiers and managers. QR-first ordering, live kitchen boards, role-scoped dashboards and clean reporting.

Our mission

Cut the friction between a guest scanning a QR code and the kitchen getting to work - while giving managers clear signals about what's actually happening on the floor.

What we care about

Speed on the floor

Every screen is designed to be used under pressure, one-handed, during service.

Role-scoped access

Guests, waiters, cooks, cashiers, managers and admins see only what they need.

Real-time everything

Orders, table statuses and queues update live across every screen via SignalR.

Made with care

We ship small, consistent improvements weekly - based on what crews actually ask for.
