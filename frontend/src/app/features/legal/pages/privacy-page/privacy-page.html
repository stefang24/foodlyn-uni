Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use Foodlyn, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

We collect:

  • Account information - name, email, username, hashed password, optional profile picture, and the role assigned to your account.
  • Order information - the items you order, modifiers, notes, totals, payment method, and timestamps.
  • Session and device data - anonymous session identifiers, cookies needed to keep you signed in, and limited technical data such as user agent.
  • Location data - temporarily, only when scanning a QR code, to verify you're physically at the restaurant. Coordinates are evaluated server-side and not retained.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Operate the Service - place and track orders, run kitchens, manage staff.
  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • Improve and troubleshoot the platform.
  • Communicate with you about your account, orders, and any service updates.

3. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of the contract you enter into with us when you create an account, our legitimate interest in running and improving the Service, and your explicit consent where required by law.

4. Sharing your data

We share your data with the restaurants that need it to fulfil your order (e.g. items, table number, party size). We do not sell your data to advertisers. We may share data with service providers who help us operate Foodlyn under appropriate confidentiality terms.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use first-party cookies that are strictly necessary for the Service to function - most importantly, the authentication cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Data retention

We retain account and order data for as long as needed to provide the Service and meet our legal and tax obligations. Inactive accounts are archived after a reasonable period.

7. Your rights

You may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@foodlyn.app.

8. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, store passwords as salted hashes, and follow the principle of least privilege in our staff access. No system is 100% secure, so please help by using a strong, unique password.

9. Children

Foodlyn is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than where you live. We apply appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect your data in those cases.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be highlighted in-app.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@foodlyn.app.

See also our Terms of Use.
